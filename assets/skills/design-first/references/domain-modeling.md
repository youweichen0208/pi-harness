# Domain terms
Read the project's existing GLOSSARY.md and domain documentation. Preserve established meanings; identify genuine new concepts or conflicting semantics.
Propose additions or changes inside the change directory, with examples and impact on interfaces/data. Ordinary variable names do not all belong in the glossary.
Keep accepted ADRs intact. A superseding proposal links the previous decision and preserves its rationale. M1 publication is reviewed manual merging under project conventions, not a transactional publication service.
